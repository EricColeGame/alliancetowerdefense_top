## Common Mistakes and What Not to Believe

Player experience reports repeatedly show the same avoidable errors. Fix these before you blame the mode:

- Assuming Hard is always the newest mode tag. The picker may use Medium, Crazy, or Insane instead.
- Pasting Hard or Insane into the code box. Those are labels, not active promo codes.
- Queueing a public lobby before a one-player probe. A vote can drop you into a wave you cannot fund.
- Buying x2 Luck or VIP Pass expecting a Hard unlock. They do not skip difficulty.
- Copying a thumbnail without checking the full walkthrough. A “Hard” clip may be Crazy.
- Trusting auto-clear scripts. Community reports note no public auto-clear listing for this experience at last check, and scripts can violate platform rules.
- Forcing exact wave counts from unrelated games. Alliance Tower Defense does not publish a public Hard wave table in the reference material.

The better habit is simple: confirm the row, confirm the map, queue alone, and change one variable after each clean finish. That routine is boring, but it protects your coins and gives you readable results.
