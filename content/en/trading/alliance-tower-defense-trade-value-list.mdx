## How Demand, Status, and Rarity Affect Value

No Alliance Tower Defense trade value list can capture everything in one number. Demand and status are the context that turns a value into a real trade. Use the demand score as a measure of how quickly players want the item, not as a guarantee of profit.

| Demand Score | What It Usually Means | Trading Implication |
|---:|---|---|
| 10/10 | Exceptional interest | Expect competition; avoid lowball offers |
| 8–9/10 | Strong interest | Good liquidity; adds may still be needed |
| 6–7/10 | Moderate interest | Fair trades are possible with patience |
| 4–5/10 | Soft interest | Expect longer waits or added items |
| 1–3/10 | Very low interest | Hard to move; value may be theoretical |

Status labels are just as important. “Dropping” means the market is cooling. “Overpaid” means current offers can exceed the listed value. “Unstable” means the price is moving unpredictably. “Obsolete” means demand has largely moved on. These labels are community shorthand, not official game terms.

| Status | Practical Reading | What to Do |
|---|---|---|
| Stable | Value is holding | Use the list as a fair anchor |
| Dropping | Sellers are accepting less | Avoid buying at old highs |
| Overpaid | Buyers are paying above list | Compare recent offers before accepting |
| Fluctuating | Price swings both ways | Get adds or wait for clarity |
| Unstable | Market is unpredictable | Double-check demand and recent trades |
| Obsolete | Interest has faded | Do not treat listed value as liquid |

In player experience, a 10/10 demand item often moves faster than a higher-value item with a 4/10 score. That does not mean demand always beats value, but it does mean liquidity deserves a place in every trade decision.

Rarity adds another layer. Omega items sit at the top of the snapshot, but rarity alone does not guarantee demand. Event items can be scarce or plentiful depending on timing. Exclusive items may have dedicated collectors. Mythic and Legendary items often fill the middle of the market. The safest approach is to combine rarity, demand, and status instead of relying on one label.

## Smart Trading Rules and Example Offers

Use the Alliance Tower Defense trade value list as a starting point, not a final answer. The best traders compare value, demand, status, and recent offers before they accept. Here is a practical checklist.

- Confirm the exact item name and variant. Similar names can have very different values.
- Check demand before value. A 10/10 demand item is usually easier to trade than a 4/10 item.
- Read the status. “Dropping” means do not buy at yesterday’s high.
- Add up both sides with a calculator, then ask whether the offer is liquid.
- Avoid accepting a pile of low-demand items for one clean, high-demand item.
- Set a walk-away number before you enter the trade.
- Verify game details and item existence on the [official Roblox games directory](https://www.roblox.com/games/) before trusting a stranger’s claim.

One more rule: do not chase “overpaid” labels blindly. An overpaid item can be a great sell and a terrible buy. If Large Camera Prototype is fluctuating and players are overpaying today, that does not mean the next buyer will do the same. Check recent trade ads and value history before you assume the premium will last.
