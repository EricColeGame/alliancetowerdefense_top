## How to Read Demand and Status Tags Correctly

Demand is the most underrated column on any value list, and it is the single best predictor of whether a trade will actually close. Community reports describe demand as a measure of how many players are actively hunting an item right now, not how strong the item is in gameplay.

| Demand Rating | What It Means | How to Trade Around It |
|---|---|---|
| 10/10 | Almost everyone wants one | Expect overpays; buying cheap is very hard |
| 8–9/10 | Strong, steady interest | Fair-value trades close within minutes |
| 6–7/10 | Moderate interest | Negotiate; bundles work well |
| 4–5/10 | Niche or collector-only | Expect lowballs and slow sales |

Status tags are the second half of the puzzle. They tell you where the price is heading, not where it has been.

| Status Tag | Interpretation | Practical Move |
|---|---|---|
| Dropping / Dropping slowly | Value is bleeding out | Move it sooner rather than later |
| Overpaid | Buyers are paying above list | Good moment to sell |
| Underpaid | Sellers are accepting below list | Good moment to buy |
| Fluctuating / Unstable | Price swings both ways | Cross-check a second source |
| Obsolete | Demand has largely dried up | Avoid as the centerpiece of a trade |

## Five Mistakes That Quietly Drain Your Value

- **Trading by item count instead of value.** Three items worth 600 combined is not a fair swap for one item worth 1,750.
- **Ignoring the trend column.** Buying a "Dropping" item at full list price is buying a depreciating asset.
- **Overvaluing rarity labels.** A Mythic with 4/10 demand is often worth less than an Event item with 8/10 demand.
- **Skipping the value history.** A single snapshot can hide a steep two-week slide.
- **Accepting the first offer in a crowded server.** Scarcity of attention is not scarcity of supply.

## FAQ

**Are Alliance Tower Defense trading values the same as in-game prices?**
No. Listed values are community and tracker estimates of relative worth. Actual in-game prices move with supply, demand, and how motivated a specific trader is, so treat the list as a negotiation baseline.

**How often do Alliance Tower Defense trading values change?**
Frequently — some items shift daily. High-demand items like Poseidon Cameraman can be re-priced within hours of a new update, so re-check the list before any trade above a few hundred value.

**What does "Overpaid" mean on a value list?**
It means buyers are currently paying more than the listed number to secure the item. That is a sell signal, not a warning about the item itself.

**Is the most expensive item always the best trade target?**
Rarely. The top item in the current snapshot is flagged as dropping slowly, while several cheaper items carry stronger demand. Value plus demand plus trend beats raw value almost every time.

**Should I use a trade calculator for small trades?**
Yes, especially for bundles. Small items are where value quietly leaks — five 100-value adds feel generous but rarely equal a single 700-value piece.
