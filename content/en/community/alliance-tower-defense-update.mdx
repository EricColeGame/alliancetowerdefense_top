### Balancing Your Team

Placement and composition still decide most matches. A team of high-rarity units with no synergy loses to a well-placed mid-tier lineup almost every time. Focus on:

- **Coverage** — units that hit different enemy types so nothing walks through untouched.
- **Placement** — corners and choke points over long straight lines.
- **Upgrade order** — invest in the units doing the most consistent damage first.
- **Flexibility** — keep one slot for whatever the weekly update makes strong.
